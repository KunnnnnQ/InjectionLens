Week 4 - Relations and closures

A relation on a set is a subset of the Cartesian product. Reflexive, symmetric and transitive closures are the three constructions we use most often in this course.

Worked example: the transitive closure of a finite relation can be computed by repeated composition until the relation stops growing.
