15-Minute Tomato Pasta

A weeknight classic. Serves 2.

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 400 g canned tomatoes
  • Salt, pepper, and basil to taste

Boil the pasta in well-salted water. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil and gently cook the garlic until fragrant. Add the tomatoes, simmer 8 minutes, and season. Toss with the drained pasta and finish with basil.
